# Code of Conduct

Gleip contributors are expected to collaborate professionally and respectfully.

## Standards

- Be direct, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent, but prioritize clarity when expectations differ.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language.
- Respect maintainer decisions about scope, quality, and release timing.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and comments that do not meet these standards. Serious or repeated violations may result in restricted participation.
